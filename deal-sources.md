# TagDrop deal sources - evidence trail

Every price on the TagDrop site was read by hand on Friday 27 November 2026
(Asia/Jakarta) on one of the pages below, in a re-research pass that
replaced the first version of the data file. Nothing was scraped in bulk
and no price was invented: a deal was only kept when a fetched page named
the product, the store and the price, and gave a link to the product page
at that store. All times are WIB (Asia/Jakarta) on 27 Nov 2026.
